Covington, Virginia Maria Hernandez

Born 1955 in Tampa, FL

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida
Nominated by George W. Bush on April 20, 2004, to a seat vacated by Ralph W. Nimmons, Jr. Confirmed by the Senate on September 7, 2004, and commission issued on September 10, 2004. Assumed senior status on July 12, 2020.

Education:
University of Tampa, B.S., 1976
University of Tampa, M.B.A., 1977
Georgetown University Law Center, J.D., 1980

Professional Career:

Trial attorney, Federal Trade Commission, 1980-1981

Assistant state attorney, Thirteenth Judicial Circuit, Florida, 1982-1983

Assistant U.S. attorney, Middle District of Florida, 1983-2001; Civil Division, 1983-1988; chief, Asset Forfeiture Section, Criminal Division, 1989-2001

Judge, Florida District Court of Appeal, Second District, 2001-2004
