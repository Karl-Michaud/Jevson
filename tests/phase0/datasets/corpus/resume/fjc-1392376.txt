Miller, Gray Hampton

Born 1948 in Houston, TX

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Texas
Nominated by George W. Bush on January 25, 2006, to a seat vacated by Ewing Werlein, Jr. Confirmed by the Senate on April 25, 2006, and commission issued on April 25, 2006. Assumed senior status on December 9, 2018.

Education:
University of Houston, B.A., 1974
University of Houston Law Center, J.D., 1978

Professional Career:

Private practice, Houston, Texas, 1978-2006
