(1) Tommy Tailgate Lift and (1) Genie SLA-10 Lift

Overview

This lot contains (1) Tommy tailgate lift and (1) Genie SLA-10 lift. Both items are in repairable condition. The equipment has been stored outdoors for more than one year and requires repairs. (47651G-6258-0019)

Specifications

Tommy Tailgate Lift:

Make: Eaglelift Palfinger

Model: EDL-60

Serial Number: E52035

Genie SLA-10:

Make: Genie

Model: SLA-10

Key Features

Tommy lift includes hydraulic platform with diamond plate deck surface.

Genie SLA-10 features aluminum mast construction with wheeled base.

Condition & Markings

This lot is in repairable condition. Parts are missing, and repairs are required.

Both lifts show weathering and corrosion from extended outdoor storage.

Tommy lift platform exhibits rust staining and surface deterioration.

Genie lift displays paint wear and oxidation on metal surfaces.
