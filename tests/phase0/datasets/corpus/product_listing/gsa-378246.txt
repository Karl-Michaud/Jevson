Internal Hard Drives

Overview

This lot contains 34 2.5 inch internal hard drives. The lot is in unused condition and includes Toshiba-IMSourcing MQ01ACF050 500GB hard drives, one Toshiba HDD2L04 disk drive, and one Seagate laptop thin HDD 320GB. The MQ01ACF050 drives feature 7mm height design, 7,278 rpm speed, and 6Gbit/s SATA interface with 12ms average seek times. The drives include shock sensor circuit protection up to 3,430 m/s2 during operation and carriage lock mechanism providing protection up to 8,820 m/s2 when powered down.
