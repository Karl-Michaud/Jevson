Fenner, Gary A.

Born 1947 in St. Joseph, MO

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Western District of Missouri
Nominated by William J. Clinton on December 13, 1995, to a seat vacated by Scott Olin Wright. Confirmed by the Senate on July 10, 1996, and commission issued on July 25, 1996. Assumed senior status on September 8, 2015.

Education:
University of Kansas, B.A., 1970
University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Law, J.D., 1973

Professional Career:

Private practice, Platte City, Missouri, 1973

Assistant city attorney, St. Joseph, Missouri, 1973-1977

Business law instructor, Webster College, 1976-1977

Private practice, St. Joseph, Missouri, 1977-1979

Judge, Circuit Court of Missouri, Fifth Judicial Circuit, 1979-1987

Judge, Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District, 1988-1996; chief judge, 1994-1996
