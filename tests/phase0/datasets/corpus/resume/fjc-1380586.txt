Ezra, David Alan

Born 1947 in Columbus, OH

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the District of Hawaii
Nominated by Ronald Reagan on November 18, 1987, to a seat vacated by Samuel P. King. Confirmed by the Senate on May 19, 1988, and commission issued on May 20, 1988. Served as chief judge, 1999-2005. Assumed senior status on June 27, 2012.

Education:
St. Mary's University, Texas, B.B.A., 1969
St. Mary's University School of Law, Texas, J.D., 1972

Professional Career:

U.S. Army Reserve, 1971-1977

Law clerk, Office of the Corporation Counsel, City and County of Honolulu, Hawaii, 1972

Private practice, Honolulu, Hawaii, 1972-1988

Adjunct professor of law, William S. Richardson School of Law, 1978-

Member, Judicial Conference of the United States, 2003-2005
