Guilford, Andrew J.

Born 1950 in Santa Monica, CA

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Central District of California
Nominated by George W. Bush on January 25, 2006, to a seat vacated by Dickran M. Tevrizian. Confirmed by the Senate on June 22, 2006, and commission issued on June 26, 2006. Assumed senior status on July 5, 2019. Service terminated on January 31, 2020, due to retirement.

Education:
University of California, Los Angeles, A.B., 1972
University of California, Los Angeles, School of Law, J.D., 1975

Professional Career:

Private practice, Costa Mesa, California, 1975-2006
