Pryor IV Pole Lot

Overview

Lot contains (5) Pryor Products Model 176 six-leg IV pole. This lot is in used condition.

Key Features

25-inch diameter six leg steel laser base.

3-inch white dual precision sealed bearing casters with 10-year warranty.

Telescoping stainless steel slow descending inner pole.

Stainless steel 8 hook top for line management.

Compressed height 68 inches from floor.

Fully extended height 99 inches from floor.

Condition & Markings
