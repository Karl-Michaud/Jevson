Barrett, Michael Ryan

Born 1951 in Cincinnati, OH

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Ohio
Nominated by George W. Bush on December 16, 2005, to a seat vacated by Walter Herbert Rice. Confirmed by the Senate on May 1, 2006, and commission issued on May 5, 2006. Assumed senior status on February 15, 2019.

Education:
University of Cincinnati, B.A., 1974
University of Cincinnati College of Law, J.D., 1977

Professional Career:

Administrative hearing officer, State of Ohio, 1977-1978

Assistant and chief assistant prosecutor, Hamilton County, Ohio, 1978-1984

Private practice, Cincinnati, Ohio, 1984-2006
