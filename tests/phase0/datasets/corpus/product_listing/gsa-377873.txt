HP Ink Cartridges

Overview

HP toner yellow and black 650A cartridges. This lot is in new condition. The cartridges are unused with unopened boxes and packaging. Compatible with LaserJet CP5520/CP5525DN/CP5525N/CP5525XH/M750DN/M750N/M750XH printers. The lot includes one box of yellow toner (part number CE272A / 650A) and one box of black toner (part number CE270A / 650A).
