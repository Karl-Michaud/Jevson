Seybert, Joanna

Born 1946 in Brooklyn, NY

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of New York
Nominated by William J. Clinton on September 24, 1993, to a new seat authorized by 104 Stat. 5089. Confirmed by the Senate on November 20, 1993, and commission issued on November 24, 1993. Assumed senior status on January 13, 2014.

Education:
University of Cincinnati, B.A., 1967
St. John's University School of Law, J.D., 1971

Professional Career:

Trial attorney, Legal Aid Society, New York City, 1971-1973

Senior trial attorney, Federal Defender Services, Legal Aid Society, Brooklyn, New York, 1973-1975

Private practice, Woodbury, New York, 1976, 1979

Senior staff attorney, Legal Aid Society, Nassau County, New York, 1976-1979

Chief, Major Litigation Bureau, Nassau County [New York] Attorney's Office, 1980-1987

Judge, Nassau County [New York] District Court, 1987-1991

Judge, Nassau County [New York] Court, 1992-1993

Other Nominations/Recess Appointments:

Nominated to U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of New York, July 27, 1992; no Senate vote
