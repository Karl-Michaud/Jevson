Van Sickle, Frederick L.

Born January 31, 1943, in Superior, WI
Died September 2, 2021

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Washington
Nominated by George H.W. Bush on March 21, 1991, to a new seat authorized by 104 Stat. 5089. Confirmed by the Senate on May 9, 1991, and commission issued on May 14, 1991. Served as chief judge, 2000-2005. Assumed senior status on May 1, 2008. Service terminated on September 2, 2021, due to death.

Education:
University of Wisconsin, B.S., 1965
University of Washington School of Law, J.D., 1968

Professional Career:

U.S. Army first lieutenant, JAG Corps, 1968-1970

Prosecutor, Douglas County, Washington, 1971-1975

Private practice, Washington, 1970-1975

Judge, Superior Court of the State of Washington, 1975-1992; Grant and Douglas Counties, 1975-1979; Chelan and Douglas Counties, 1979-1992
