Selna, James V.

Born 1945 in San Jose, CA

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Central District of California
Nominated by George W. Bush on January 29, 2003, to a seat vacated by J. Spencer Letts. Confirmed by the Senate on March 27, 2003, and commission issued on March 27, 2003. Assumed senior status on March 3, 2020.

Education:
Stanford University, A.B., 1967
Stanford Law School, J.D., 1970

Professional Career:

U.S. Army Reserve captain, 1967-1978

Private practice, California, 1970-1998

Judge, Superior Court of California, County of Orange, 1998-2003
