HP Color LaserJet CP3525dn Printer

Overview

HP Color LaserJet CP3525dn Printer with model CC470A designation. This network-ready automatic duplex color laser printer delivers speeds up to 30 pages per minute for both black and color printing. The unit is in used condition.

Specifications

Make: Hewlett Packard

Model: CP3525dn

Key Features

Print resolution up to 600 x 1200 dpi.

Paper handling capacity includes 100-sheet multipurpose tray 1 and 250-sheet tray 2 for 350 sheets standard capacity.

Standard automatic two-sided printing capability.

Hi-Speed USB 2.0 and embedded HP Jetdirect Ethernet networking connectivity.

384 MB RAM expandable up to 1 GB.

Monthly duty cycle up to 75,000 pages.

Dimensions 20.2 inches wide x 19.3 inches deep x 14.1 inches high with 85 pound weight.

Condition & Markings

This lot is in used condition. Parts may be missing, and repairs may be required.
