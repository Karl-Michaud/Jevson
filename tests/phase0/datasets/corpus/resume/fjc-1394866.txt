Rossiter, Robert F., Jr.

Born 1956 in Camp Lejeune, NC

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the District of Nebraska
Nominated by Barack Obama on June 11, 2015, to a seat vacated by Joseph F. Bataillon. Confirmed by the Senate on June 27, 2016, and commission issued on June 29, 2016. Served as chief judge, 2021-2026.

Education:
Purdue University, B.S., 1978
Creighton University School of Law, J.D., 1981

Professional Career:

Law clerk, Hon. Albert G. Schatz, U.S. District Court, District of Nebraska, 1981-1982

Law clerk, Hon. C. Arlen Beam, U.S. District Court, District of Nebraska, 1982-1983

Adjunct faculty, Creighton University School of Law, 1983-1987, 1999-

Private practice, Omaha, Nebraska, 1983-2016
