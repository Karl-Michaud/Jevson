Konica Minolta Bizhub C3350 Printer

Overview

Konica Minolta bizhub C3350 compact A4 color multifunction laser printer. The unit is in used condition.

Key Features

Copies, prints, and scans up to 35 pages per minute in both full color and black and white.

7-inch tablet-like touchscreen interface.

Built-in Emperon Print System.

Up to 1200 x 1200 dpi resolution.

Standard 650-sheet paper capacity expandable to 1,650 sheets.

50-sheet document feeder and 100-sheet bypass tray.

Mobile printing support including Apple AirPrint, Mopria, and NFC.

Energy Star certified with energy-efficient biomass-based Simitri HD toner.

Dimensions: 21.9" x 22.9" x 23.7" (W x D x H).

Weight: approximately 105 lbs.

Includes new toner in box.

Condition & Markings

This lot is in used condition. Parts may be missing, and repairs may be required.
