Savage, Timothy J.

Born 1946 in Philadelphia, PA

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania
Nominated by George W. Bush on March 21, 2002, to a seat vacated by Edward N. Cahn. Confirmed by the Senate on August 1, 2002, and commission issued on August 1, 2002. Assumed senior status on March 1, 2021.

Education:
Assumption College, B.A., 1968
Temple University School of Law (now James E. Beasley School of Law), J.D., 1971

Professional Career:

Private practice, Pennsylvania, 1971-2002

Hearing examiner, Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board, 1976-2002
