HP Thunderbolt Dock G2 Docking stations

Overview

This lot contains 4 HP Thunderbolt Dock G2 docking stations model HSN-IX01. Used condition. The docking station is designed for workspace flexibility and network manageability, delivering USB-C device connectivity and optional integrated audio. Each unit includes the HSN-IX01 docking station and AC adapter.

Key Features

All-in-one docking station with dual 4K resolution support.

USB-C port with data and power output (15W).

Computer interface via USB-C connection.

Multiple connectivity options including VGA (1), DisplayPort (2), Ethernet (1), USB 3.0 (3), USB-C (1), and Thunderbolt 3 (1).

3.5mm audio connectivity.

Compatible with HP Elite x2 1012 G1/G2, HP Pro x2 612 G2, HP EliteBook Folio G1, HP EliteBook x360 1030 G2/1020 G2, HP EliteBook 1040 G4, HP EliteBook 800 G5/700 G5, HP ProBook 600 G4/400 G5, ZBook 14u G5, and ZBook 15u G5.
