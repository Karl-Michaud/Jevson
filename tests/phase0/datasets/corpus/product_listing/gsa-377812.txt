Digital Signage Media Player

Overview

VideoTel VP70LTE plus industrial-grade looping digital signage media player. This lot is in unused condition.

Key Features

Auto powers on, auto-plays, auto loops without manual interaction.

HDMI resolution support: 720p, 720i, 1080p, 1080i.

Multiple video output options: HDMI, NTSC, PAL.

Stereo L/R audio output at line level.

Supports multiple video formats: .avi .vob .dat .iso .mkv .mov .mp4 .mpg .mpeg1 .mpeg2 .ts .mets .wmv .rm .rmvb .flv.

Compatible video codecs: h.263, h.264, mpeg1, mpeg2, mpeg4, wmv9, vc-1, rmvb.

Photo slideshow with transition style options.

Compact dimensions: 3 1/8" (W) x 2 1/4" (D) x 9/16" (H).

Designed for continuous 24/7 operation with rugged use capability.

Low failure rate of less than 1/3 of 1% after 6 years of use.

Lightweight design: player alone weighs 2.4oz, complete package weighs 13.7oz.
