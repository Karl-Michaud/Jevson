Fuller, Mark E.

Born 1958 in Enterprise, AL

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Alabama
Nominated by George W. Bush on August 1, 2002, to a seat vacated by Ira DeMent. Confirmed by the Senate on November 14, 2002, and commission issued on November 26, 2002. Served as chief judge, 2004-2011. Service terminated on August 1, 2015, due to resignation.

Education:
University of Alabama, B.S., 1982
University of Alabama School of Law, J.D., 1985

Professional Career:

Private practice, Alabama, 1985-1996

Chief assistant district attorney, Twelfth Judicial Circuit, Alabama, 1996-1997

District attorney, Twelfth Judicial Circuit, Alabama, 1997-2002
