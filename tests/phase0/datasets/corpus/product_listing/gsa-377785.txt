Dymo LabelWriter 400 Turbo Thermal Label Printer

Overview

Dymo LabelWriter 400 Turbo thermal label printer. This item is in used condition.

Key Features

Prints labels up to 2-1/4" wide.

55 labels per minute print speed.

Direct thermal printing without ink or toner.

300 dpi resolution.

USB connectivity.

Windows and Macintosh compatible.

Compact design with quiet operation.
