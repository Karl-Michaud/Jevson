Overview

This lot contains (7) generators in repairable condition. The lot includes (3 ea.) diesel-powered 2kw units and (4 ea.) gas-powered generators. Working condition is unknown and repairs may be required. (47651G-6258-0009)

Key Features

Predator 9000 generators with 7500 running watts and 8750 peak watts.

Predator 4375 generators with 3500 running watts and 4375 starting watts.

Some military diesel generators with control panels featuring voltage meters, frequency meters, and convenience receptacles rated at 120VAC, 60HZ, 15 AMPS.

Diesel generators with protective housing and multiple electrical outlets.

Condition & Markings

Working condition of all generators is unknown.

Red and black color scheme on Predator generators.

Military green color on diesel generators.
