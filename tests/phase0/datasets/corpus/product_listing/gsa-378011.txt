( 2) Ski-Doo Snowmobiles

Overview

This lot contains (2) Ski-Doo snowmobiles. These units are reported to be in repairable condition by the owning agency and recommended to be considered for parts due to numerous missing components. (47651G-6258-0023)

Model Year: 2013

Engine: 600HO ETEC

VIN: 2BPSGLDA6DV000015

Condition & Markings

This lot is in repairable condition. Parts are missing, and repairs are required.

Both snowmobiles have extensive impact damage to body panels, hoods, and windshields.

Multiple body panels are cracked, broken, or missing.

One unit has exposed engine compartment with visible engine components.

Track systems and suspension components are present on both units.

Instrument clusters and gauges are visible but condition is compromised.
