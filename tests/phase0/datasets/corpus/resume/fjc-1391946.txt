McKnight, H[arold] Brent

Born February 20, 1952, in Mooresville, NC
Died November 27, 2004, in Charlotte, NC

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Western District of North Carolina
Nominated by George W. Bush on April 28, 2003, to a new seat authorized by 116 Stat. 1758. Confirmed by the Senate on July 31, 2003, and commission issued on August 1, 2003. Service terminated on November 27, 2004, due to death.

Other Federal Judicial Service:
U.S. Magistrate Judge, U.S. District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, 1993-2003

Education:
University of North Carolina, B.A., 1974
University of Oxford, Magdalen College, M.A., 1976
University of North Carolina School of Law, J.D., 1980

Professional Career:

Assistant district attorney, State of North Carolina, 1982-1988

Judge, North Carolina District Court, Twenty-Sixth Judicial District, 1989-1993

Visiting lecturer, University of North Carolina at Charlotte, 1992-2003

Adjunct assistant professor, Wingate University, 1994-2003
