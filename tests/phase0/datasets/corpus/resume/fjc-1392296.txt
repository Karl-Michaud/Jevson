Mattice, Harry Sandlin, Jr.

Born 1954 in Chattanooga, TN

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee
Nominated by George W. Bush on July 28, 2005, to a seat vacated by R. Allan Edgar. Confirmed by the Senate on October 24, 2005, and commission issued on November 18, 2005. Assumed senior status on March 10, 2020. Service terminated on September 30, 2021, due to retirement.

Education:
University of Tennessee, B.S., 1976
University of Tennessee College of Law, J.D., 1981

Professional Career:

Private practice, Tennessee, 1981-2001

Senior counsel, U.S. Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs, 1997

U.S. attorney for the Eastern District of Tennessee, 2001-2005
