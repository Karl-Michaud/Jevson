Snowplow Blades, Qty 3

Overview

This lot contains (3) snowplow blades in repairable condition. The units have been stored outdoors for over a year and require maintenance. (47651G-6258-0017)

Key Features

Yellow painted blade assemblies with black hydraulic mounting systems.

Spring-loaded cutting edge mechanisms for ground contour following.

Hydraulic angle adjustment cylinders for directional snow control.

Heavy-duty steel construction with reinforced mounting brackets.

Condition & Markings

This lot is in repairable condition. Parts may be missing, and repairs may be required.

Extended outdoor storage has resulted in surface weathering and corrosion on metal components.

Hydraulic systems show signs of exposure with potential seal deterioration.

Paint finish shows fading and wear from weather exposure.

Units have been exposed to outdoor elements for over twelve months.
