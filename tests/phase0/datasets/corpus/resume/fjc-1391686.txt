Klausner, Robert Gary

Born 1941 in Los Angeles, CA

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the Central District of California
Nominated by George W. Bush on July 18, 2002, to a seat vacated by William D. Keller. Confirmed by the Senate on November 14, 2002, and commission issued on November 15, 2002.

Education:
University of Notre Dame, B.A., 1963
University of Notre Dame, B.S., 1964
Loyola Law School, Los Angeles, California, J.D., 1967

Professional Career:

U.S. Army, 1967-1969

Deputy district attorney, Los Angeles County, California, 1969-1974

Commissioner, Pasadena [California] Municipal Court, 1974-1980

Judge, Pasadena [California] Municipal Court, 1980-1985

Judge, Superior Court of California, County of Los Angeles, 1985-2002
