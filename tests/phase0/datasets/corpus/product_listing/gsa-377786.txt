ScanSnap S1300i

Overview

This lot contains (6) ScanSnap S1300i document scanners. Used condition. The ScanSnap S1300i is a compact, portable color duplex document scanner that features a 10-sheet automatic document feeder and scans up to 12 pages per minute.

Key Features

Scan speed up to 12 ppm / 24 ipm using AC power.

10-sheet automatic document feeder.

Up to 600 dpi optical resolution.

USB bus-powered or AC adapter power options.

PC and Mac compatibility.

Cloud integration with services like Dropbox, Google Drive, and Evernote.

Condition & Markings
