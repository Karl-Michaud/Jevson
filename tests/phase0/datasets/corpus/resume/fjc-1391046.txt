Hochberg, Faith S.

Born 1950 in East Orange, NJ

Federal Judicial Service:
Judge, U.S. District Court for the District of New Jersey
Nominated by William J. Clinton on April 22, 1999, to a seat vacated by Joseph H. Rodriguez. Confirmed by the Senate on November 10, 1999, and commission issued on November 15, 1999. Service terminated on March 6, 2015, due to retirement.

Education:
Tufts University, B.A., 1972
Harvard Law School, J.D., 1975

Professional Career:

Law clerk, Hon. Spottswood Robinson, U.S. Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit, 1975-1976

Special assistant to the chairman, U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, 1976

Private practice, Newark, New Jersey, 1977-1983, 1987-1990

Assistant U.S. attorney, District of New Jersey, 1983-1987

Senior deputy chief counsel, Office of Thrift Supervison, U.S. Department of the Treasury, 1990-1993

Deputy assistant secretary for law enforcement, U.S. Department of the Treasury, 1993-1994

U.S. attorney for the District of New Jersey, 1994-1999

Other Nominations/Recess Appointments:

Nominated to U.S. District Court for the District of New Jersey, November 27, 1995; no Senate vote
